Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026.

The Old Gods Atlas app is used only by the owner of the Old Gods Atlas YouTube channel to upload, schedule and update that channel's own videos. Nobody else signs in to it.

It uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies.

The app reads and writes only the channel's own video information (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, privacy and schedule). It collects no information about viewers or anyone else, shares nothing with third parties, and uses no cookies or analytics. Its sign-in token is kept on the owner's computer and nowhere else.

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which the token stops working.
